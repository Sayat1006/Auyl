# Style reference (what the user's reference reels do)

Analysed reference: Kazakh history explainer, 576×1024 30 fps, 45.8 s, ~16 scenes of 2.5–3.5 s, only 4 hard cuts.

- **Narrator**: user's face cut out with a thick white sticker outline; big and centred at the top in the hook, then small bottom-left. Puppet jaw: the chin below the lip line drops with the voice. Emoji reactions next to the head on emotional beats (😳 😢 😮).
- **Headlines**: very bold uppercase sans, centred in the top third; line 1 white, line 2 accent (yellow positive, red negative). Words appear one after another.
- **Chips**: rounded pills with emoji icons («🎫 «Өз еркімен келді»», «барақ · жертөле», «🌾 КҮРІШ ШАРУАШЫЛЫҒЫ ↑»).
- **Devices**: red stamp slam with shake (myth-bust, year on a document); big counters («47 000+»); day boxes filling («БІР АПТА»); two name chips joined by an arc («ұстаз → шәкірт»); document paper «ҚАУЛЫ · ПОСТАНОВЛЕНИЕ».
- **Continuity**: elements persist across scenes (the train crosses several scenes); backgrounds change softly → use `cut:"soft"` for scenes that continue the same place, circle wipe for chapter changes.
- **Colour mood**: hardship = dark blue-grey / brown, drifting snow/dust; humanity = warm sunset orange with a big sun disc; achievement = bright sky + green fields.
- **Subtitles**: dark rounded box at the bottom, karaoke (grey → white), keywords yellow/red.
- **Audio**: continuous voice, ~0.3 s pauses, very quiet music bed.
- Never copy another creator's watermark/handle; use the user's own tag or none.
